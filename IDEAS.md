# Case ideas

Solved cases with one clear "last mistake". VERIFY every fact from sources fetched on the day before using one.
Mark a line `[used <id>]` when it becomes a Reel. Prefer non-violent cases (heists, cons, hackers, fugitives).

- [used 001] Silk Road: Ross Ulbricht. An early "altoid" forum post with his real Gmail address.
- AlphaBay: Alexandre Cazes. His personal Hotmail address appeared in the site's welcome/password-reset emails.
- LulzSec: Hector Monsegur ("Sabu"). Logged into chat once without hiding his IP address.
- Antwerp Diamond Centre heist (2003): Leonardo Notarbartolo's crew. A half-eaten sandwich and rubbish dumped by a road.
- Hatton Garden heist (2015): the "Diamond Wheezers". A distinctive white Mercedes caught on CCTV and traced.
- Bitfinex hack: Ilya Lichtenstein and Heather Morgan. Stolen coins traced to accounts in their own names.
- Hushpuppi (Ramon Abbas): an Instagram life of luxury that let investigators match accounts and places.
- Kevin Mitnick: tracked through his mobile phone signal after hacking a security researcher.
- Victor Lustig: "sold" the Eiffel Tower; caught later over counterfeit money.
- The Bling Ring: posted the stolen goods and hangouts on social media.
- Albert Gonzalez (TJX card theft): an informant link to his online alias.
